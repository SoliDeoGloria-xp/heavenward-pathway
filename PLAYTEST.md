# Playtest checklist (0.2.0)

Play on a phone in landscape. Note the time at each ★.

**Start**
- [ ] Title → age gate (try a year under 13: refused) → create a pilgrim (try the name "Lord": refused)
- [ ] The dream cards read well; Skip works

**Eden Isle** ★ target ~34 min, ends Base 5 / Job 2
- [ ] Every coach step points at the right control and never blocks you
- [ ] Name the lamb; Word Study; both secrets findable (✦ 2/2)
- [ ] The Fall: serpent, darkening, cherubim, the lamb stays behind. Nothing shows God or the man and woman

**City of Destruction** ★ ~20 min, Base 6 / Job 4
- [ ] You wake under the burden (Burden chip, slower walk)
- [ ] Learn First Steps; Evangelist appears; Snapshot unlocks
- [ ] Obstinate (optional) and the Dreamer's Page are findable
- [ ] Let yourself be KO'd once: Weariness, no XP lost
- [ ] Sitting is not interrupted by new foes

**Slough and Wicket Gate** ★ ~15 min, Base 7 / Job 5
- [ ] Mire slows and drains (never below 30% Health); stones are safe
- [ ] Try both Worldly Wiseman answers on two runs
- [ ] The Gate scene reads clearly: received, not achieved

**Interpreter's House** ★ ~15 min
- [ ] Five tableaux; Word Study; Prayer of Rest appears in Skills

**The Cross** ★ ~10 min, Base 8 / Job 6
- [ ] Burden falls and rolls into the tomb; Burden chip gone; full speed
- [ ] Only the cross, the tomb and light are shown

**Hill Difficulty** ★ ~20 min, Base 9 / Job 8
- [ ] Both field bosses: red circles are fair to dodge
- [ ] Arbour choice; go back for the roll; lions hurt only off the middle of the path

**Palace Beautiful** ★ ~25 min, Base 10 / Job 10
- [ ] Armor of God shows in Equipment
- [ ] Calling trial: the lent skill is on bar page 2
- [ ] Lions' Path, then the Muster with Lumen, Rook and the Steward
- [ ] The lamb's bell; choose a Calling; robe colour and name plate change

**Gear and the House (0.3.0)**
- [ ] Foes drop Denarii and sometimes gear; "Wear the best" works
- [ ] Provisioner (City) and Steward (Palace): buy, sell, store
- [ ] Armory bench after the Armor is given: refine to +5, cut and set a Fruit stone
- [ ] Codex: bestiary fills in; daily portion can be received once

**Valley of Humiliation** ★ ~30 min
- [ ] Ranged Spites throw from afar; pride falls faster with more Wisdom
- [ ] Apollyon: the dialogue, the volleys below half Health, the leaves afterwards

**Valley of the Shadow of Death** ★ ~30 min
- [ ] The ditch and the quag punish leaving the path, fairly
- [ ] Staff does nothing to fiends; All-Prayer does, and costs no Zeal
- [ ] The voice, daybreak, Faithful

**Whole game**
- [ ] ★ Total time to the Calling (target ~2 h 20 min)
- [ ] Quit mid-map, reopen, Continue: same objective, same XP
- [ ] Settings: text size Large and Largest, left-handed, reduce motion, volumes
- [ ] On a Sunday: Lord's Day panel appears; tester bypass works
- [ ] No text under 16 pt, no clipped panels, safe areas respected on your phone's notch
- [ ] Theology review: every dialogue scene in `data/dialogue.json`

## Added in 0.5.0: the look

- [ ] Hold the phone upright on any map: the HUD re-lays itself, nothing is cut off, every button is reachable with a thumb. Turn it back.
- [ ] Open Status, Skills, Satchel, the shop and a conversation upright: text is readable and nothing spills off the edges.
- [ ] Walk each map end to end. Note any prop that floats, sinks, hides the pilgrim or blocks the path where nothing is drawn.
- [ ] Note any picture that looks smoother, blurrier, larger-pixelled or differently lit than the things around it.
- [ ] Walk in a circle and turn the camera: the view should glide, never jerk. Note the device and refresh rate if it does.
- [ ] Is the parchment interface readable in daylight? Is any text too small?

## Added in 0.6.0 phase 1: roads between maps

- [ ] In the Slough, walk west into the small glow: you are in the City again, with no quest. Walk east into the shining light: back in the Slough, at the same objective.
- [ ] After Goodwill opens the Wicket Gate, walk through it on foot to the light beyond. (Before this build the gate was a wall.)
- [ ] Finish the story, press "Walk the road again", and walk back to the City and forward again. Note any portal that is hidden by scenery, hard to reach, or sets you down inside something.
- [ ] Does any portal take you when you did not mean it to? Does the label say clearly where it leads?
- [ ] At the Palace after the Calling: the yard, the Lions' Path and the Muster board each lead to their side map again and bring you back beside them.
- [ ] Continue a save made in 0.5.0: you are where you were, and the way back is open.

## Added in 0.6.0 phase 2: towns, fields and danger zones

- [ ] From the City, take the south road to the City Square. Trade, open the chest, rest at the well, read the board, speak to the three townsfolk.
- [ ] Use the Milestone to go to another town and back. Is it clear what a Milestone is and that it is free?
- [ ] Walk into Sloth Meadow at a low level, then into a field far above you. Do the portal sign and the nameplate colours warn you in time?
- [ ] Strike one foe in a pack marked alert (Gossips in Neighbours' Lane): do its fellows come? Do passive foes leave you alone?
- [ ] Find a field's landmark, its hidden cache and its Dreamer's Page. Are they too easy or too hard to notice?
- [ ] In a danger zone, step on the bad ground, go down the stairs, wake the boss. Fall on purpose: you should wake in the last town, weary, with no XP lost.
- [ ] Note every new map that looks empty, and every prop that blocks a road or hides a portal. These maps are plain kit-built placeholders.
- [ ] Theology review: the names and lines listed under "Awaiting theological review" in DECISIONS.md.

## Added in 0.6.0 phase 3: levelling, Gleanings and the save file

- [ ] **Save file.** Hunt in a field for a minute, then swipe the app away without using any menu. Open it and press Continue: are you on the same spot with the same XP? Try again by pressing Home, waiting, and coming back.
- [ ] More → Settings: does it show the slot and "last saved"? Does "Save now" answer at once?
- [ ] Take a Gleaning at a town board, overcome 20 foes in that field, receive it. Is it clear where to hand it in? Take it again.
- [ ] After the Calling, time one level between Base 10 and 13 in a field at your level. The plan says 12 to 18 minutes. Faster or slower, and by how much?
- [ ] Hunt foes five levels below you and five above. Does the difference in XP feel fair?
- [ ] Does the goals board read as something that waits for you, not something you can miss?

## Added in 0.6.0: menus

- [ ] Scroll Settings, the Guide and the Codex with your thumb starting on a button or a row. Does it scroll every time? Does anything get pressed by accident?
- [ ] Satchel: tap tiles, use bread, wear and take off a piece. Is it clear what is worn ("E") and what each tile is?
- [ ] Equipment: tap each slot. Is "better / worse than what you wear" enough to decide by?
- [ ] At a Provisioner: buy, sell, store and take back. Are the tiles big enough? Is the price easy to read?
- [ ] Which other windows still feel like plain lists (Skills, Status, the Way, Codex, the town board)? Name them and they will be next.

## Added in 0.6.0 phases 4 and 5: world map and field life

- [ ] Open the world map from More and by tapping the minimap. Drag it. Is it clear where you are and where you have not been?
- [ ] At a Milestone, travel to another town. Does the window fit your screen held both ways?
- [ ] Does the minimap help you find the ways out of a field?
- [ ] Stay in a field for a minute: the named foe should come and show on the minimap. Too strong, too weak, too often?
- [ ] Gather a fig and honey, cook at a town's pot, eat the result.
- [ ] After the story's end, enter a field: Faithful should walk with you and strike what you strike. Is he in the way?
- [ ] Turn Auto on in a field, then try it in a town and a danger zone.
- [ ] After the Calling, spend skill points over several Job Levels. Do you always have something to want, without feeling starved?
