# Playtest checklist (0.2.0)

Play on a phone in landscape. Note the time at each ★.

**Start**
- [ ] Title → age gate (try a year under 13: refused) → create a pilgrim (try the name "Lord": refused)
- [ ] The dream cards read well; Skip works

**Eden Isle** ★ target ~34 min, ends Base 5 / Job 2
- [ ] Every coach step points at the right control and never blocks you
- [ ] Name the lamb; Word Study; both secrets findable (✦ 2/2)
- [ ] The Fall: serpent, darkening, cherubim, the lamb stays behind. Nothing shows God or the man and woman

**City of Destruction** ★ ~20 min, Base 6 / Job 4
- [ ] You wake under the burden (Burden chip, slower walk)
- [ ] Learn First Steps; Evangelist appears; Snapshot unlocks
- [ ] Obstinate (optional) and the Dreamer's Page are findable
- [ ] Let yourself be KO'd once: Weariness, no XP lost
- [ ] Sitting is not interrupted by new foes

**Slough and Wicket Gate** ★ ~15 min, Base 7 / Job 5
- [ ] Mire slows and drains (never below 30% Health); stones are safe
- [ ] Try both Worldly Wiseman answers on two runs
- [ ] The Gate scene reads clearly: received, not achieved

**Interpreter's House** ★ ~15 min
- [ ] Five tableaux; Word Study; Prayer of Rest appears in Skills

**The Cross** ★ ~10 min, Base 8 / Job 6
- [ ] Burden falls and rolls into the tomb; Burden chip gone; full speed
- [ ] Only the cross, the tomb and light are shown

**Hill Difficulty** ★ ~20 min, Base 9 / Job 8
- [ ] Both field bosses: red circles are fair to dodge
- [ ] Arbour choice; go back for the roll; lions hurt only off the middle of the path

**Palace Beautiful** ★ ~25 min, Base 10 / Job 10
- [ ] Armor of God shows in Equipment
- [ ] Calling trial: the lent skill is on bar page 2
- [ ] Lions' Path, then the Muster with Lumen, Rook and the Steward
- [ ] The lamb's bell; choose a Calling; robe colour and name plate change

**Whole game**
- [ ] ★ Total time to the Calling (target ~2 h 20 min)
- [ ] Quit mid-map, reopen, Continue: same objective, same XP
- [ ] Settings: text size Large and Largest, left-handed, reduce motion, volumes
- [ ] On a Sunday: Lord's Day panel appears; tester bypass works
- [ ] No text under 16 pt, no clipped panels, safe areas respected on your phone's notch
- [ ] Theology review: every dialogue scene in `data/dialogue.json`
